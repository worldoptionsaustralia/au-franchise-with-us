Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need logistics experience?

Logistics, freight or transport experience is preferred but not essential. Strong B2B sales, relationship-building and business-development skills matter most, and training covers the platform and the sales process.

Do I need trucks, a warehouse or inventory?

No. The model is relationship and technology driven. Carriers move the freight and Head Office manages the platform and operations. Other start-up and operating costs apply and are explained in the discovery process.

What training and mentoring are provided?

Initial training, platform onboarding, sales-process guidance and marketing materials, followed by ongoing mentoring, coaching and growth planning. See the Support section for the four stages.

Can I operate from home?

Many franchise partners work from a home office or on the road with a laptop and phone. You will still meet customers and prospect actively, and any territory or local requirements are covered in the discovery process.

Which carrier and freight services are available?

The platform offers domestic and international parcels, pallets, express and economy services through multiple carriers. Services can change over time, and the current carrier list is shared in the discovery process.

What happens after I submit an enquiry?

A member of the franchise team reviews your details and contacts you to arrange an introductory call at a time that suits you. You then receive further franchise information and details of the discovery process. There is no obligation at any stage.
